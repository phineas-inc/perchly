About

Perchly is a concept project: an interactive prototype of a cloud-based mission control platform that helps small-satellite teams monitor telemetry, send commands and schedule ground station passes.

It was designed and built by Tanishq as a portfolio piece. It is not a real company or service, and nothing on this site is an offer.
